S.M.A.R.T. CubeSat

Soil Moisture Assessment & Response Tracking — University Nanosatellite Program

The S.M.A.R.T. satellite was designed and developed by a team of 7 aerospace engineers at North Carolina State University. As Project Manager, I led those engineers through the entire development process.

A detailed set of functional requirements laid the foundation for the satellite. The satellite was built with a very limited budget, with a final price tag of around $1,200 — trailblazing cheap satellite development.

The satellite was created in response to devastating flooding in western North Carolina. S.M.A.R.T. utilized synthetic aperture radar to detect objects hundreds of meters away. This data was then wirelessly transmitted to a ground station that developed a map of the affected terrain.
