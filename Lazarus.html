⚠ Read the numbers honestly

Every figure here comes from backtesting and paper (simulated) trading — not a record of real, executed capital. Backtested results are hypothetical and can flatter a strategy in hindsight. The one-week run from $500 to $1,613.35 is an extremely small sample on a deliberately high-risk 0DTE strategy — driven far more by variance and a handful of outliers than by a proven edge, and runs like it routinely reverse.

Paper trading omits the frictions that decide whether a strategy works: live slippage, the pattern-day-trader rule, a delayed feed, and the discipline cost of real money. None of this is investment advice. Past and simulated performance does not predict future results, and 0DTE options can lose their entire value within a single trading day.

How It Works

Synchronized 5m / 15m feeds across seven underlyings, run through custom TA pipelines, reversal logic, and automated risk management.

01 / signal

Multi-timeframe engine

Reads 5m and 15m OHLCV structure together — EMA crossovers, VWAP positioning, RSI momentum, abnormal volume — so signals must agree across timeframes before firing.

02 / detect

Signal intelligence

Detects breakout continuation, VWAP rejections, failed opening-range breaks, RSI divergences, and EMA reclaim/loss structures — split into momentum and reversal setups.

03 / execute

Execution layer

Selects ATM and far-OTM 0DTE contracts from the broker's own option chain, sizes against a shared daily budget, and confirms the actual fill before tracking the trade.

04 / manage

Automated risk management

Runs each position live with volatility-tiered stops and targets, scaled profit-taking, breakeven runner stops, and a hard end-of-day close — verifying every exit on the broker.

How It Was Built

step 01

Signal research & strategy design

Started with a thesis: intraday momentum and exhaustion leave a readable footprint across timeframes. Defined the rules — multi-timeframe EMA structure, VWAP positioning, RSI, volume expansion — for two opposite ideas: breakout continuation and far-OTM reversal.

step 02

Backtesting & replay framework

Before risking anything, built a replay engine to run the rules over 45 days of historical data, producing trade-by-trade P&L and analytics. This is where the simulated figures come from — useful for pressure-testing logic, but hypothetical by nature.

step 03

Real-time bot architecture

The strategy split into two independent Discord bots — a momentum "Main" engine and an asymmetric "Lotto" engine — each scanning live feeds, calling out setups, and dispatching structured alerts the moment a signal fires.

step 04

Live execution via Alpaca

Wired the bots into Alpaca's paper-trading API so signals became actual orders — contract selection, position sizing against a shared daily budget, fills, and account-level P&L — turning a scanner into a system that trades.

step 05

Grounding everything in real data

The hardest phase: replaced every internally-estimated price with live brokerage data. Confirmed fill prices on entry, real unrealized P&L on exit, position-truth reads on every command, and contract selection from the broker's own chain — so the strike chosen and the price reasoned about come from the same feed that fills the trade.

step 06

Risk & trade management

Added volatility-tiered stops and targets, scaled profit-taking — trim the bulk at the first target, let one or two runner contracts ride with the stop at breakeven — and focused new entries into the highest-movement part of the session.

step 07

Honest analytics

Built reporting that measures the strategy rather than flatters it: per-leg accounting that separates trimmed legs from runners, and a time-of-day study that finds when the underlying actually moves — surfacing the truth about the edge, including when there isn't one.

step 08

Autonomous operations

Finally, made it run itself: scheduled with systemd, fully operable from a phone over a private mesh network and owner-locked Discord admin commands, with self-deploy and a watchdog that auto-rolls-back a bad update if the bot stops sending heartbeats.
