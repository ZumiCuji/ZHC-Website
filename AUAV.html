Automated Unmanned Aerial Vehicle

A custom-built RC aircraft integrated with a full autopilot system for autonomous waypoint navigation.

A fellow aerospace engineering student and I set out to build a radio-controlled (RC) airplane from essential parts. To prepare for the maiden flight—and avoid turning it into airborne debris—I spent countless hours on a flight simulator. The development of the manned airplane was relatively straightforward and mostly plug-and-play.

The test flights were successful but left the aircraft with significant damage along the way. At one point, a faulty battery fried multiple flight controllers, requiring us to install and configure new hardware.

Integrating the autopilot system proved to be the real challenge. GPS and compass calibration issues, combined with quirks in the Mission Planner software, demanded extensive troubleshooting. In the end, our persistence paid off — the plane successfully executed its first fully preprogrammed mission.
